Output
expression in unit — once, at the end. Use GB or KB/year. For rates, in seconds means per second; for durations, it means seconds. Explicit in 1/second also works. Put arithmetic before in.

Singular, plural, and uppercase all work: month, months, month(s), MONTH. A month is 30 days; a year is 365 days. KB, MB, GB, and TB use powers of 1,000. B means billion; use bytes for bytes. DAU and MAU are treated as rates for capacity estimates; converting them does not estimate distinct active users.

    Downtime = period × (1 − availability). Uses a 30-day month and 365-day year. This is a time-based budget; a request-based SLO measures the share of unsuccessful requests instead.

    What makes the target credible?

    Replication Separate failure domains

    Copies help only when they do not share the same failure. Discuss zones, replication lag, quorum requirements, and the latency cost of synchronous writes.

    Failover Detection + recovery time

    Explain how failure is detected, who becomes the leader, and how traffic moves. Include detection delays, false positives, split brain, and degraded capacity.

    Recovery RTO + RPO

    RTO is how long recovery may take; RPO is how much recent data may be lost. Replication is not a backup: test restores and recovery procedures.
